NOVO ACADEMY course overview

The NOVO ACADEMY is the free online training of Novogenia GmbH for partners, consultants and professionals. In 22 courses, Dr. Daniel Wallerstorfer explains the science behind Novogenia analyses, how the result reports are structured and the questions clients ask most. Each course ends with a test, and at the end you receive a Genetics Coach certificate.

Frequently asked questions about the academy

How much does the NOVO ACADEMY cost?
Nothing. Registration and all courses are free.
Who is the NOVO ACADEMY for?
For partners and resellers who offer Novogenia analyses, for professionals such as nutritionists, coaches, pharmacists and physicians, and for anyone who wants to understand how genes influence nutrition, performance and appearance.
How does a course work?
Each course consists of videos, downloadable materials and a test. A test is passed with at least 80 percent correct answers. Your progress is saved in your account.
What is the Genetics Coach certificate?
Anyone who passes the module tests receives a personal certificate from Novogenia GmbH as a PDF. It confirms the completed training modules and is not a state-recognised professional qualification.
In which languages are the courses available?
In 11 languages: German, English, Czech, French, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Romanian, Spanish, Serbian and Arabic. Videos that were translated into another language with AI are labelled as such directly below the video.
